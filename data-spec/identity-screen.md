## The identity screen: wording

The first screen, before the welcome (W). It follows the survey's copy rules: plain British English, short lines, no
promise of anonymity (answers are linked to the person, as the design intends).

| Element | Wording |
|---|---|
| Heading | Before you start |
| Line under the heading | Enter your SID and your name. Your answers are saved as you go, so you can stop and come back to them. |
| Spaces typed before or after the SID or name | Removed without a message |
| First field: label | Your SID |
| First field: hint in the box | For example R123456 |
| Second field: label | Your full name |
| Button | Continue |
| Link at the foot | Privacy (opens the notice from J.P. Morgan privacy and HR, as on the welcome) |
| Error: SID left empty | Enter your SID. |
| Error: SID with a space or another character inside it | Use letters and numbers only, for example R123456. |
| Error: SID longer than 20 characters | That's too long for an SID. Check it and try again. |
| Error: name left empty | Enter your name. |
| Error: name longer than 200 characters | That's too long. Shorten it to 200 characters or fewer. |
| Returning, survey in progress | Welcome back. Your answers are saved, so you can carry on where you left off. Button: Carry on |
| Returning, answers already sent | You've already sent your answers. Thank you. |

If the survey runs behind J.P. Morgan single sign-on, skip this screen. Take the SID and name from the session and
show them on the welcome, for example "Signed in as R123456, Jane Smith", rather than asking for them.
